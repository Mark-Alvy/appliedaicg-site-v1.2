Privacy policy

Effective November 2026.

Applied AI Consulting Group ("AACG", "we") operates appliedaicg.com. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form or book a call, we receive the information you enter: your name, work email, company, and message. Form submissions are processed by Formspree and delivered to our email. Call bookings are processed by Google Calendar appointment scheduling.

We use privacy respecting analytics that do not set cookies and do not track you across sites. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for their marketing.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, schedule and prepare for calls, and keep basic records of our conversations. We keep contact information for as long as there is an ongoing business relationship or a reasonable prospect of one.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing markalvarez@appliedaicg.com.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and note the effective date above.
